The customer details window puts one customer in one place: their data, the latest surat jalan sent to them, and the buttons to edit or deactivate them.

<Roles can={["OWNER", "ADMIN", "GUDANG", "PENJUALAN", "VIEWER"]} />

## Opening a customer

On the **Customers** page, click the customer's row. The details open over the list. Click **Close** to go back to the list.

<Shot name="customers-detail" alt="Details of Pangkalan Bu Sri with the customer data and the five latest surat jalan" />

The top part shows the customer data. Empty fields are not shown; in the picture, NPWP and notes are missing because they were never filled in.

## Recent surat jalan

The **Recent surat jalan** table <span class="pin">1</span> lists up to 10 of the latest surat jalan for this customer, newest first.

| Column | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Number** | The surat jalan number. A draft has no number yet, so it reads **Draft**. |
| **Date** | The surat jalan date |
| **Status** | **Draft**, **Posted** or **Cancelled** |
| **Quantity** | Total tabung on that surat jalan |
| **kg** | Total kg on that surat jalan |

To see all of this customer's surat jalan, open **Surat Jalan** in the sidebar and filter by customer, see [Surat jalan list and export](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/daftar/).

## Editing and deactivating

The **Edit** and **Deactivate** buttons only appear for Owner, Admin and Sales. **Edit** opens the customer form, see [Adding and editing customers](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/pelanggan/menambah/).

### Deactivating a customer

Customers cannot be deleted. When you no longer serve a customer, deactivate them instead.

<Steps>

1. Open the customer's details and click **Deactivate**.

2. GasDist asks you to confirm: the customer can no longer be chosen for new transactions, but their history is kept. Click **Deactivate** to go ahead, or **Cancel**.

</Steps>

| | Inactive | Active |
|---|---|---|
| Shown in the customer list | Only with **Show inactive** ticked | ✅ |
| Can be chosen on a new surat jalan | ❌ | ✅ |
| Old surat jalan and kartu stok | Stay intact | Stay intact |

### Reactivating

In the customer list, tick **Show inactive**, open the customer's details, then click **Activate** and confirm. The customer can be chosen on a surat jalan again straight away.

<Aside type="note">
A draft surat jalan for a customer who has been deactivated cannot be saved or posted. Reactivate the customer, or change the customer on the draft.
</Aside>
