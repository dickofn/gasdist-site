The **Customers** page holds everyone you deliver tabung to: pangkalan, retail shops, industry and others. From here you search for customers, open their details and add new ones.

<Roles can={["OWNER", "ADMIN", "GUDANG", "PENJUALAN", "VIEWER"]} />

Every role can see and search customers. Only Owner, Admin and Sales see the **Add customer** button and can change customer data.

## Reading the customer list

Open **Customers** in the sidebar. The list is sorted by name.

<Shot name="customers-list" alt="Customer list with columns for code, name, type, city, phone and status" />

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Add customer** <span class="pin">1</span> | Opens the new customer form, see [Adding and editing customers](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/pelanggan/menambah/) |
| **Search** <span class="pin">2</span> | Searches by code, name, phone or city |
| **Type** <span class="pin">3</span> | Shows one customer type only |
| **Show inactive** <span class="pin">4</span> | Also lists customers that have been deactivated |

Click a row to open the [customer details](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/pelanggan/detail/).

## Searching for a customer

<Steps>

1. Type part of a code, name, phone number or city in **Search** <span class="pin">1</span>. The list filters as you type; there is no need to press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

2. If needed, choose one type under **Type** <span class="pin">2</span>. In the picture, the word `bekasi` with the type **Pangkalan** leaves four pangkalan in Bekasi.

   <Shot name="customers-search" alt="Searching bekasi with type Pangkalan shows four pangkalan" />

3. To get the full list back, clear the search and choose **All types**.

</Steps>

If nothing matches, **No matching customers** appears. Try a shorter word, or check the **Type** filter.

## Customer types

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Pangkalan** | An LPG pangkalan selling to households |
| **Retail** | A small shop or kiosk |
| **Industry** | A business that uses the gas itself, e.g. catering, laundry, a bakery |
| **Other** | Anything outside the three above, e.g. a cooperative canteen |

## Inactive customers

Inactive customers are hidden from the list. Tick **Show inactive** to see them; the **Status** column then shows **Inactive**. How to deactivate and reactivate a customer is in [Customer details](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/pelanggan/detail/).

<Aside type="note">
The list shows at most 500 customers at a time. If you have more, a message says how many are shown. Narrow the search to find the rest.
</Aside>
