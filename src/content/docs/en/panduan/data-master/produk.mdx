A product is a kind of tabung you sell, for example LPG 3 kg or LPG 12 kg. Stock is kept per product, and the product's capacity says how many kg of gas are in one tabung.

<Roles can={["OWNER", "ADMIN"]} />

The **Master Data** menu only appears for Owner and Admin.

## The product list

Open **Master Data** in the sidebar. The **Products** tab opens first; the **Vehicles** and **Drivers** tabs sit next to it <span class="pin">1</span>.

<Shot name="master-products" alt="The Products tab in Master Data with four LPG products" />

Each row shows the code, name, capacity, **Low-stock threshold (kg)** <span class="pin">3</span>, order and status. The **Actions** column holds **Edit** and **Deactivate**.

## Adding a product

<Steps>

1. Click **Add product** <span class="pin">2</span>.

2. Fill in the fields (see the table below), then click **Save**.

</Steps>

## Editing a product

<Steps>

1. Click **Edit** on the product's row.

2. Change what you need. Under each kg field, GasDist repeats the number it read, for example `= 5.5 kg`, so you can be sure it was understood correctly.

   <Shot name="master-product-edit" alt="Edit LPG 5,5 kg form with a capacity of 5.5 kg and a low-stock threshold of 165 kg" />

3. Click **Save**.

</Steps>

| Field | Required? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Product code** | ✅ | 1 to 20 letters, digits, dots, underscores, hyphens or slashes, no spaces. Must differ from every other product. |
| **Product name** | ✅ | The name shown on screen and printed on the surat jalan |
| **Capacity (kg)** <span class="pin">1</span> | ✅ | The gas in one full tabung, in kg. Used to turn tabung counts into kg. |
| **Empty tabung weight (kg)** | | The weight of the tabung without gas, for reference. May be left blank; it is not used in any stock calculation. |
| **Low-stock threshold (kg)** <span class="pin">2</span> | ✅ | When stock falls below this, the product's card on the Home page shows **Low**. Enter 0 to turn it off. |
| **Order** | ✅ | Smaller numbers come first, on the Home page and in product lists |

For kg fields, use a dot for decimals, for example `5.5`.

## The low-stock threshold

The threshold is in kg, not tabung. To work it out, multiply the minimum number of tabung by the capacity. Example: you want a warning when LPG 5,5 kg drops below 30 tabung, so enter 30 × 5.5 = `165`.

The **Low** badge appears on the product's card on the [Home page](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/stok/ringkasan/) and in the **Stock after** column while [making a surat jalan](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/membuat/).

## Changing the capacity

The capacity can still be changed after a product has been used. What happens:

| What is affected | After the capacity changes |
|---|---|
| Posted surat jalan | Unchanged. Each line keeps the capacity it was posted with. |
| Draft surat jalan | Use the new capacity when posted |
| Stock in kg | Unchanged |
| The `= … tabung` figure on the Home page | Recalculated with the new capacity |
| Later receipts and stock opname in tabung | Calculated with the new capacity |

The product code and name are kept on each line at posting too, so renaming a product never changes an old surat jalan.

<Aside type="caution">
Change the capacity only to fix a typo. If you start selling a different tabung size, add a new product rather than changing the old one. That keeps the stock and history of the two sizes apart.
</Aside>

## Deactivating a product

Products cannot be deleted. A product you no longer sell is deactivated: click **Deactivate** on its row and confirm. To bring it back, click **Activate** on the same row.

| | Inactive product |
|---|---|
| Can be chosen on a surat jalan or in Receive stock | ❌ |
| Shown on the Home page | Only while it still holds stock, with an **Inactive** badge |
| Kartu stok in the Reports menu | ✅, marked **(Inactive)** |
| Old surat jalan and history | Stay intact |
