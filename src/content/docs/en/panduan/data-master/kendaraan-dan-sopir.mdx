Registered vehicles and drivers can be chosen when making a surat jalan, and are printed on it. Neither is required: a surat jalan can still be made with **No vehicle** and **No driver**.

<Roles can={["OWNER", "ADMIN"]} />

## Vehicles

<Steps>

1. Open **Master Data** in the sidebar, then click the **Vehicles** tab.

2. Click **Add vehicle** <span class="pin">1</span>.

   <Shot name="master-vehicles" alt="The Vehicles tab with three active vehicles" />

3. Fill in the **Plate number**, for example `B 9123 KXA`, and, if useful, a **Description**, for example the type of vehicle and its load. Click **Save**.

</Steps>

A plate number may contain letters, digits, spaces or hyphens, up to 15 characters, and is always written in capitals.

## Drivers

<Steps>

1. In **Master Data**, click the **Drivers** tab.

2. Click **Add driver** <span class="pin">1</span>.

   <Shot name="master-drivers" alt="The Drivers tab with three active drivers" />

3. Fill in the **Driver name** (required) and **Phone** (optional). Click **Save**.

</Steps>

To correct a vehicle or driver, click **Edit** on its row.

<Aside type="caution">
A surat jalan shows the current plate number and driver name, including when an old surat jalan is printed again. Use **Edit** only to fix typos. When a vehicle is replaced or a new driver joins, add a new entry and deactivate the old one.
</Aside>

## Deactivating a vehicle or driver

Vehicles and drivers cannot be deleted. When a vehicle is sold or a driver leaves, deactivate them instead.

<Steps>

1. Click **Deactivate** on the vehicle's or driver's row.

2. Read the confirmation: the entry can no longer be chosen for new transactions, but its history is kept. Click **Deactivate**.

   <Shot name="master-deactivate" alt="Confirmation dialog to deactivate Supriyadi" />

</Steps>

The row stays in the list with the status **Inactive**. To use it again, click **Activate** on the same row.

| | Inactive |
|---|---|
| Can be chosen on a new surat jalan | ❌ |
| Old surat jalan that used it | Stay intact and can still be printed |
| Drafts that still use it | Cannot be saved or posted until it is changed, or reactivated |

<Aside type="tip">
Don't know the vehicle or driver yet when making a surat jalan? Choose **No vehicle** or **No driver**, save it as a draft, and fill them in before posting. See [Making a surat jalan](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/membuat/).
</Aside>
