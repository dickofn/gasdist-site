GasDist keeps a distributor's LPG stock in kg and its surat jalan in tabung. It works fully **offline** on one PC: no internet is needed, and all data stays on that PC, **encrypted**. This page walks you through the main window so you know where to find each feature. New to it? [Quick start](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/mulai-cepat/) takes you from installing to one surat jalan delivered.

## The main window

After you sign in, GasDist always opens the **Home** page. The main window has a sidebar menu on the left and a top bar on the right.

<Shot name="home" alt="The GasDist main window: sidebar menu on the left, company name and account buttons at the top, Home with the today's surat jalan card and a stock card per product in the middle" />

| Part | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Sidebar menu <span class="pin">1</span> | Moves between pages. Which items you see depends on your [role](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/peran/). The GasDist version is shown at the bottom; the same version is in **Settings › About**, where the Owner [updates GasDist](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/memperbarui/). |
| Company name | On the left of the top bar, for example **PT Sumber Gas Sejahtera**. The Owner changes it in [Settings](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/pengaturan/perusahaan/); while a license is installed, it follows the license. |
| **Trial · *n* days left** | Only without a license: the trial's days left, then **Trial ended** (read only). The Owner clicks it to add the license. See [License and trial](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/lisensi/). |
| **Language** <span class="pin">2</span> | Choose **Bahasa Indonesia** or **English**. The choice is saved for your own account, so other users are not affected. |
| **Theme** | **System**, **Light** or **Dark**. Also saved for your own account; see [Light and dark theme](#light-and-dark-theme). |
| Name and role | Who is signed in, for example **Hendra Wijaya · Owner**. |
| **Change password** | Changes your own password. See [Signing in, locking and passwords](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/masuk-dan-kunci/). |
| **Lock** <span class="pin">3</span> | Locks the screen while you are away from your desk. You stay signed in; your password opens it again. |
| **Sign out** | Ends your session and returns to the sign-in screen. |

Home itself has the cards for today's surat jalan, the signed sheets not back yet and the backup, buttons such as **Receive stock** <span class="pin">4</span>, and one card per product. The **Low** badge <span class="pin">5</span> marks a product whose stock is below its threshold. The full story is in [Home](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/beranda/) and [Stock on Home](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/stok/ringkasan/).

## The sidebar menu

| Menu | What it is for | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | The start page: today's surat jalan, the signed sheets not back yet, backup status, each product's stock in kg and tabung, receiving stock, stok opname, the newest surat jalan | [Home](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/beranda/) |
| **Surat Jalan** | The surat jalan list; making, posting, printing and cancelling them | [Surat jalan list and export](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/daftar/) |
| **Customers** | Pangkalan and other customers, search, each customer's surat jalan history | [Customer list and search](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/pelanggan/daftar/) |
| **Master Data** | Products, vehicles and drivers | [Products](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/data-master/produk/) |
| **Reports** | Kartu stok, stock summary, surat jalan register, deliveries, surat jalan not back yet, and the audit trail (Owner and Admin); each exports and prints | [Stock summary](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/laporan/ringkasan-stok/) |

<Aside type="note">
Missing one of the menus above? That is expected: a menu is hidden when your role may not use it. See [User roles](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/peran/).
</Aside>

## Light and dark theme

Choose **Theme** in the top bar:

<Shot name="topbar-theme" alt="The top bar: Language, then Theme set to System" />

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **System** <span class="pin">1</span> | The default. GasDist follows the Windows (or Linux) theme: when the system is in dark mode GasDist turns dark too, and it changes when the system does. |
| **Light** | Always light. |
| **Dark** | Always dark. |

Everything is in the same place in every theme; only the colours change. The choice is saved for your own account on this PC, so other users of the same PC keep their own. The sign-in screen uses the theme last used on this PC. Printed sheets are always white.
