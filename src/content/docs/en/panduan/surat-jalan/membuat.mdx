A surat jalan goes with every delivery of tabung to a customer. While it is still a **draft**, you can change anything and stock does not move. Once it is **posted**, the surat jalan gets its number and stock goes down straight away.

<Roles can={["OWNER", "ADMIN", "PENJUALAN"]} />

## Filling in a new surat jalan

<Steps>

1. Open **Surat Jalan** in the side menu, then click **New surat jalan** (or press <kbd>F2</kbd>). The date is already set to today <span class="pin">1</span>; type another as dd/mm/yyyy or pick it from the calendar (see [Entering dates](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/tanggal/)). The keyboard shortcut line <span class="pin">2</span> is always shown above the form.

   <Shot name="sj-form-empty" alt="An empty new surat jalan form" marks={[1, null, 2]} />

2. Type part of the customer's code, name, phone number or city in the **Customer** field <span class="pin">1</span>. Pick from the list <span class="pin">2</span> with the up/down arrows and <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or click it.

   <Shot name="sj-form-customer" alt="The customer field showing the customers that match the word sri" />

3. Choose the **Vehicle** and **Driver** if you already know them. Both can be left empty. See [Vehicle and driver](#vehicle-and-driver) below.

4. Fill in the lines: in **Product** <span class="pin">1</span>, type part of the product's name or code and pick it with <kbd>Enter</kbd> or a click, then type the number of **Tabung** <span class="pin">2</span>. Click **Add line** for the next product; pressing <kbd>Enter</kbd> on the last line also adds a line.

   The **Stock after** column <span class="pin">3</span> shows how much of that product is left if this surat jalan is posted. The total tabung and kg <span class="pin">4</span> update as you type.

   <Shot name="sj-form-filled" alt="A surat jalan form with two lines: LPG 3 kg 200 tabung and LPG 5,5 kg 10 tabung" marks={[1, 2, 3, 4, null]} />

</Steps>

## Empty tabung back

A pangkalan usually swaps on the spot: it takes the full tabung and hands back empty ones. Record the empties handed back for each line in the **Empties back** column <span class="pin">1</span> before posting, so the count prints on the sheet the customer signs.

<Shot name="sj-form-empties" alt="A surat jalan with the Empties back column at 150 and 1, the Empties = tabung button, and the empties under the totals" />

- **Empties = tabung** <span class="pin">3</span> fills every line's **Empties back** with its tabung count. Change the lines that differ <span class="pin">2</span>, for instance when the pangkalan hands back 1 empty for 2 full tabung.
- Blank means no empties. The count may be larger than the tabung sent, when a pangkalan hands back tabung from an earlier delivery.
- Under the totals the empties coming back add up <span class="pin">4</span>.

The empties are fixed at posting, like the rest of the line. A wrong count after posting? While it is **Posted**, [cancel and make it again](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/membatalkan/#cancel--recreate). Once **Delivered**, put it right with a [tabung count](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/laporan/tabung-dipinjam/#counting-tabung). The empties are what the [Tabung on loan](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/laporan/tabung-dipinjam/) report adds up.

## Vehicle and driver

The **Vehicle** <span class="pin">1</span> and **Driver** fields work like the customer field: click, or type part of the plate or the name. The list starts with **No vehicle** <span class="pin">2</span>, then the vehicles **most recently used** on a surat jalan, then the rest A to Z. The vehicles you use every day are always at the top.

<Shot name="sj-form-vehicle" alt="The Vehicle list open: No vehicle, the most recently used vehicles, and + Add vehicle at the bottom" />

Vehicle or driver not registered yet? Type the plate, then pick **+ Add vehicle** <span class="pin">3</span> at the end of the list. The **Add vehicle** dialog opens with the plate you typed <span class="pin">1</span>. Fill in the rest if needed, then click **Save** <span class="pin">2</span>: the vehicle is saved in Master Data and picked on the surat jalan straight away. **+ Add driver** works the same way.

<Shot name="sj-add-vehicle" alt="The Add vehicle dialog with the plate B 9911 KXA typed in the Vehicle field" />

<Roles can={["OWNER", "ADMIN", "PENJUALAN"]} />

The Sales role can **add** vehicles and drivers here, but changing and deactivating them stays in [Master Data](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/data-master/kendaraan-dan-sopir/), for the Owner or an Admin.

### What the colours in Stock after mean

| Looks like | Meaning | Can you post? |
|---|---|---|
| Black, e.g. `6,234 kg` | Stock is fine | ✅ |
| Orange, `22 kg (Low)` | What is left is below that product's low-stock level | ✅, but reorder soon |
| Red, `-250 kg (Negative)` | Not enough stock for this line | ❌ the **Post** button is locked |

<Shot name="sj-form-negative" alt="The LPG 50 kg line in red with the word Negative and a message that posting is locked" />

The red line <span class="pin">1</span> comes with a message <span class="pin">2</span>, and the **Post** button <span class="pin">3</span> cannot be clicked. Lower the quantity or remove the line with the **×** button.

<Aside type="note">
The Owner can allow negative stock in [Settings](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/pengaturan/stok-dan-keamanan/). Usually it is better not to: negative stock means the records and the warehouse no longer agree.
</Aside>

## Saving a draft

Click **Save draft** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>) when the delivery is not certain yet. The message **Draft saved.** <span class="pin">1</span> appears. A draft has no number and does not reduce stock. With **Preview** <span class="pin">2</span> you can see how it will look on paper, with a DRAFT watermark. A draft cannot be printed yet: post it first so it gets a number (see [SALINAN and DRAFT](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/cetak/salinan-dan-draf/)).

<Shot name="sj-form-saved" alt="The surat jalan form after it was saved as a draft" />

## Posting

<Steps>

1. Click **Post** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd>). The app shows a summary: customer, date, contents and total.

   <Shot name="sj-post-dialog" alt="The post confirmation dialog with a summary of customer, date, contents and total" marks={false} />

2. Check the summary, then click **Post** once more. The surat jalan gets its number <span class="pin">1</span>, for example `SJ/2026/10/0031`, and its status becomes **Posted** <span class="pin">4</span>.

   <Shot name="sj-detail-posted" alt="A posted surat jalan page with its number and the Print and Cancel buttons" />

   From this page you can **Print** <span class="pin">2</span> right away or, if something is wrong, **Cancel surat jalan** <span class="pin">3</span>. The Owner and Admins also see a **Correct** button to [change the vehicle, driver or notes](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/mengoreksi/).

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="A posted surat jalan cannot be changed">
Only the vehicle, driver and notes can still be [corrected](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/mengoreksi/). Wrong quantity or wrong customer? [Cancel](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/membatalkan/) that surat jalan with **Cancel & recreate**, then fix it in the new draft. Cancelling returns the stock and stays on record, so the history can always be traced.
</Aside>

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>F2</kbd> | New surat jalan |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> | Save draft |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> | Post |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd> | Print preview |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> on the last line | Add line |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Back |
