The driver takes the surat jalan along, the receiver signs it, and the sheet comes back to the office. With the default print settings, that is the sheet labelled **Lembar 2: Kembali ke kantor** (back to the office; see [Printing a surat jalan](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/cetak/mencetak/)). Once you have that sheet, mark the surat jalan **Delivered**: record who received it, the date it was signed and, if you have one, a scan or photo of the sheet.

<Roles can={["OWNER", "ADMIN", "PENJUALAN"]} />

The sheets still out are listed in the [Surat jalan not back yet](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/belum-kembali/) report.

## Marking it delivered

<Steps>

1. Open a surat jalan with the status **Posted** <span class="pin">2</span>, from the [surat jalan list](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/daftar/) or from the **Surat jalan not back yet** report, then click **Mark delivered** <span class="pin">1</span>.

   <Shot name="sj-detail-deliver" alt="A posted surat jalan with the Mark delivered button" />

2. Fill in **Receiver's name** <span class="pin">1</span> as written in the **PENERIMA** box on the sheet. It is required, at most 100 characters.

3. Check **Date signed** <span class="pin">2</span>. It starts at today; change it if the sheet was signed on another day. It cannot be before the date of the surat jalan or after today ([Entering dates](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/tanggal/)).

4. If you have a scan or photo of the sheet, click **Choose file…** <span class="pin">3</span> and pick it. Its name shows next to the button; **Remove scan** takes it off again. The scan is optional.

   <Shot name="sj-deliver-dialog" alt="The Mark delivered dialog with the receiver's name, the date signed and a chosen scan" />

5. Click **Mark delivered** <span class="pin">4</span>.

</Steps>

The message **Surat jalan ... marked delivered.** appears, the status becomes **Delivered** <span class="pin">1</span> (green), and the **Signed sheet back** box <span class="pin">2</span> records the receiver, the date signed, who recorded it and when, and the scan.

<Shot name="sj-detail-delivered" alt="A delivered surat jalan with the Signed sheet back box and the View scan button" />

## Which scans are taken

| File | What happens |
|---|---|
| A JPG or PNG photo | Turned upright the way the phone was held, shrunk, and kept as a JPG of at most 1 MB. The photo's location and other metadata are dropped. |
| A PDF up to 1 MB | Kept as it is. |
| A PDF over 1 MB | Refused. Scan it again at a lower resolution, or take a photo of the sheet. |
| An iPhone photo (HEIC), TIFF or any other file | Refused. Save it as a JPG first. |
| Any file over 20 MB | Refused. |

<Aside type="tip" title="Taking the photo with a phone">
Hold the phone straight above the sheet, in good light, with all four corners in the picture. The signature and the stamp in the **PENERIMA** box must be readable.
</Aside>

## Viewing the scan

Click **View scan** in the **Signed sheet back** box (<span class="pin">3</span> in the picture above). A photo shows right in GasDist, for every role.

<Shot name="sj-scan-view" alt="The scan of a surat jalan signed by the receiver, with the Save a copy button" />

GasDist cannot show a PDF itself. Click **Save a copy…** <span class="pin">1</span>, save the file, then open it with the PC's PDF reader. A photo can be saved the same way.

## Delivered is final

| Once delivered | |
|---|---|
| Correcting the vehicle, driver or notes | ❌ there is no **Correct** button |
| Cancelling | ❌ there is no **Cancel surat jalan** button |
| Changing the receiver, the date or the scan | ❌ |
| Printing | ✅ marked as a copy if it was printed before |
| Stock | Unchanged: it went down when the surat jalan was posted |

<Aside type="caution" title="Check before you click">
A wrong receiver's name or a wrong scan cannot be fixed once saved. Filled tabung coming back after the customer received them are recorded with a [return](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/retur/): open the delivered surat jalan and click **Make a return**.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note" title="Where scans are kept">
Scans are kept encrypted in a `scans` folder next to the GasDist database, not inside it, so backups stay small and fast. Every backup copies the new ones. See [Making backups](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/backup/membuat-backup/#scans-of-signed-sheets).
</Aside>
