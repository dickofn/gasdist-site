A posted surat jalan cannot be changed, apart from its vehicle, driver and notes, which can be [corrected](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/mengoreksi/). When its contents are wrong or the delivery is called off, you cancel it: the stock comes back, and the number stays on record as cancelled, so the history can always be traced.

<Roles can={["OWNER", "ADMIN"]} />

The Sales role can make and post a surat jalan but cannot cancel one. If one needs cancelling, ask the Owner or an Admin.

## Cancelling a surat jalan

<Steps>

1. Open a surat jalan with the status **Posted**, for example from the [surat jalan list](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/daftar/), then click the red **Cancel surat jalan** button at the top right.

2. Write the reason in the **Reason** field <span class="pin">1</span>, for example `Pelanggan menunda pengiriman` (the customer postponed the delivery). A reason is required, up to 200 characters.

3. Type your own password, the one you sign in to GasDist with, in the **Password** field <span class="pin">2</span>.

   <Shot name="sj-cancel-dialog" alt="The Cancel surat jalan SJ/2026/10/0031 dialog with the Reason and Password fields" />

4. Click **Cancel surat jalan**. If the surat jalan has to be made again with the contents fixed, click **Cancel & recreate** <span class="pin">3</span> instead (see [below](#cancel--recreate)). Click **Cancel** if you change your mind; nothing changes.

</Steps>

If the password is wrong, the message **Wrong password.** appears and the surat jalan is not cancelled. Type it again and retry.

## After cancelling

The message **Surat jalan ... cancelled.** appears, the status becomes **Cancelled** <span class="pin">1</span>, and a red box <span class="pin">2</span> records who cancelled it, when, and why.

<Shot name="sj-detail-cancelled" alt="A cancelled surat jalan page with the Cancelled status and a note of who cancelled it and why" />

| Item | Before cancelling | After cancelling |
|---|---|---|
| Status | **Posted** (blue) | **Cancelled** (red, struck through) |
| Number | `SJ/2026/10/0031` | Still `SJ/2026/10/0031`, never reused |
| Stock | Reduced | Returned |
| Buttons | **Print**, **Cancel surat jalan** | Only **Preview** |
| Printing | Possible | No longer possible |

## Cancel & recreate

A wrong tabung count, wrong goods or the wrong customer usually means making the same surat jalan again with one fix. **Cancel & recreate** does both at once: the surat jalan is cancelled as usual, then a new surat jalan form opens with the same customer <span class="pin">2</span>, vehicle, driver, notes and goods <span class="pin">3</span>, dated today. The message at the top <span class="pin">1</span> reminds you it is a new draft.

<Shot name="sj-form-recreated" alt="A new surat jalan form holding the details of the surat jalan just cancelled, with a message that it is a new draft" />

Fix what was wrong, then click **Save draft** or **Post** below the form. The new surat jalan gets the next number when it is posted.

<Aside type="caution" title="The new draft is not saved yet">
The form is not saved until you save it. If you close it, GasDist asks first; if you discard it, the cancellation still stands and there is no new draft.
</Aside>

The **Cancel & recreate** button only appears for roles that may make a surat jalan, which are the Owner and Admin here.

### Where the stock goes back

Each line of the surat jalan returns to stock as a **Cancellation** line in the [kartu stok](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/stok/kartu-stok/), dated the day you cancel, not the date of the surat jalan. That way the records of earlier days, stok opname results included, stay as they were.

### Previewing a cancelled surat jalan

**Preview** still opens so you can read the contents. The page carries a DIBATALKAN watermark (CANCELLED when the company's default language is English), and the bar above it says **A cancelled surat jalan is not printed again.** There is no **Print** button.

<Aside type="caution" title="A cancellation cannot be undone">
A cancelled surat jalan cannot be brought back. If the delivery goes ahead after all, [make a new surat jalan](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/membuat/), or use **Cancel & recreate** in the first place; it gets the next number.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="A delivered surat jalan cannot be cancelled">
Once [marked delivered](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/menandai-terkirim/), the signed sheet exists, so the **Cancel surat jalan** button no longer appears. Tabung coming back after the customer received them are recorded with a [stock opname](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/stok/stok-opname/) and its reason.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip" title="Drafts are deleted, not cancelled">
A draft has no number and has not reduced stock, so there is nothing to cancel. The Owner, Admin or Sales role just opens the draft, clicks **Delete draft**, then clicks **Delete draft** again in the **Delete this draft?** dialog. A deleted draft cannot be brought back.
</Aside>
