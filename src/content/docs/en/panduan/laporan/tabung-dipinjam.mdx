The tabung belong to the agen. Every surat jalan sends full tabung to a customer, and a pangkalan usually hands empty ones back on the spot. The **Tabung on loan** report adds it all up per customer and product, so you know how many tabung each customer still holds.

<Roles can={["OWNER", "ADMIN", "GUDANG", "PENJUALAN", "VIEWER"]} />

## Reading the report

Open **Reports**, the **Tabung on loan** tab <span class="pin">1</span>. The report has no date range: it is the balance now, over everything recorded. Filter by **Customer** <span class="pin">2</span>, by product, or tick **Only what is still on loan**.

<Shot name="reports-loans" alt="The Tabung on loan report for one pangkalan, per product, with the Count tabung button and the exports" />

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Sent** | Full tabung that went out on posted surat jalan, less cancelled ones |
| **Empties back** | Empty tabung the customer handed back, from the [Empties back](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/membuat/#empty-tabung-back) column of the surat jalan |
| **Returned full** | Filled tabung that came back on a [return](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/retur/), less cancelled returns |
| **Counts** | The difference a [tabung count](#counting-tabung) recorded, up or down |
| **On loan** <span class="pin">4</span> | Sent − Empties back − Returned full ± Counts: the tabung still at the customer |
| **Last count** | When that customer's tabung were last counted |

Above the table, the tabung on loan per product add up. An **On loan** figure below zero is red: more came back than was recorded as sent, usually tabung from deliveries before tracking began. Count that customer's tabung.

The Excel and CSV exports <span class="pin">5</span> take every row. For printing, see [Printing and exporting reports](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/laporan/mencetak-dan-mengekspor/). One print holds at most 5,000 rows; with thousands of customers, choose one product or tick **Only what is still on loan** before printing.

A [customer's detail](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/pelanggan/detail/) also shows the tabung they hold per product.

## Surat jalan from before version 0.13.0

The empty tabung column arrived in GasDist 0.13.0. Surat jalan posted before it are not in this report, because their empties were never recorded: counting them would show every customer holding every tabung ever sent. On data from before the upgrade, the report says above the table from which day it counts.

So after upgrading to 0.13.0, record each customer's **opening count** with [Count tabung](#counting-tabung): how many tabung are at the customer that day. From then on the report follows every surat jalan, return and count.

## Counting tabung

<Roles can={["OWNER", "ADMIN"]} />

Count tabung records how many of the agen's tabung are really at a customer, like a [stock count](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/stok/stok-opname/) for the warehouse stock. Use it for the opening count, for regular checks, and to put right empties recorded wrong on a surat jalan that is already posted.

<Steps>

1. In the **Tabung on loan** report, click **Count tabung** <span class="pin">3</span>.

2. Choose the customer and the **Date** <span class="pin">1</span> of the count. **Recorded** shows what the record says at the end of that day.

3. Write the **Reason** <span class="pin">2</span>, for instance *Opening count at the pangkalan*, then enter **Counted** <span class="pin">3</span> for the products you counted. Leave the others blank.

   <Shot name="loans-count" alt="The Count a customer's tabung dialog with the date, the reason, and the Recorded and Counted columns per product" />

4. Click **Save count** <span class="pin">4</span>. The difference goes into **Counts** <span class="pin">2</span> and the day into **Last count** <span class="pin">3</span>.

   <Shot name="reports-loans-counted" alt="The Tabung on loan report after the count was saved, with the difference under Counts and the day of the last count" />

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
A count that matches the record is still saved, as a difference of 0, so its day shows. Every count is in the [audit trail](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/laporan/jejak-audit/) with its reason and who recorded it.
</Aside>

## Tabung in the warehouse

This report is only about tabung at customers. Empty tabung in the warehouse and the exchanges at the SPBE are not recorded: GasDist's [stock](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/stok/ringkasan/) is the weight of gas in kg.
