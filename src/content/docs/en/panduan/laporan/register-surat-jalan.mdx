The surat jalan register is the book of surat jalan numbers: every surat jalan that got a number in one period, by date then number, cancelled ones included. Use it to check that no number is missing, and to tick off the sheets that have come back.

<Roles can={["OWNER", "ADMIN", "GUDANG", "PENJUALAN", "VIEWER"]} />

## Opening the register

<Steps>

1. Click **Reports** in the sidebar, then the **Surat jalan register** tab <span class="pin">1</span>.

2. This month's register shows at once. Change **From** and **To** for another period, a year at most; in the picture, **From** is set to today, so only today's surat jalan show, with every status. The sentence above the table <span class="pin">2</span> gives how many numbered surat jalan there are, how many were shipped (with their tabung and kg), and how many were cancelled.

   <Shot name="reports-register" alt="Surat jalan register for one day with the status, customer, tabung, weight and note of each surat jalan" />

</Steps>

## What the register holds

| Status | In the register? | Added up? |
|---|---|---|
| **Posted** | ✅ | ✅ as shipped |
| **Delivered** | ✅, with the receiver's name and the date signed under **Note** <span class="pin">4</span> | ✅ as shipped |
| **Cancelled** <span class="pin">3</span> | ✅, struck through, with the reason it was cancelled | ❌, counted apart: its goods never left |
| **Draft** | ❌: a draft has no number yet | ❌ |

Cancelled surat jalan stay listed so their numbers do not drop out of the sequence. A surat jalan number is never used twice and never skipped.

Click a row to open that surat jalan. The back button at the top left brings you back to the register.

<Aside type="note">
The screen shows the first 1,000 rows. The totals above the table, the export and the print always cover every surat jalan in the period.
</Aside>

## Exporting and printing

**Export xlsx**, **Export csv** and **Print** <span class="pin">5</span> include every row, then the totals per status and what was shipped at the end. One print holds at most 5,000 rows; for a longer register, narrow the dates. See [Printing and exporting reports](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/laporan/mencetak-dan-mengekspor/).
