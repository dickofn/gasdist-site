GasDist records every change: surat jalan created, posted, corrected or cancelled, stock received, settings changed, users signing in, and so on. The audit trail shows those records, newest first, with what changed.

<Roles can={["OWNER", "ADMIN"]} />

Other roles do not see the **Audit trail** tab.

## Opening the audit trail

<Steps>

1. Click **Reports** in the sidebar, then the **Audit trail** tab.

2. This month's entries show, 200 at a time, newest first.

3. Narrow them with **User** <span class="pin">1</span>, **Action** <span class="pin">2</span> and **Entity** <span class="pin">3</span>, for example Entity **Surat jalan** for everything that happened to surat jalan.

   <Shot name="reports-audit" alt="The audit trail filtered on the Surat jalan entity, with the time, user, action, surat jalan number and changes" />

4. When older entries match too, **Load 200 more** shows under the table. Click it to add the next 200.

</Steps>

## Reading an entry

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Time** | Date and time on this computer |
| **User** | Who did it. **(system)** for what happened with nobody signed in, such as a restore from backup. |
| **Action** | What was done, with the entity under it, for example **Surat jalan corrected** on **Surat jalan** |
| **Record** | Which record: the surat jalan number, username, product or customer code, plate number, driver name or receipt reference |
| **Changes** <span class="pin">4</span> | Every field that changed: the field's name, the old value struck through, then the new value. A field that is new carries a **new** tag. |

An example, a surat jalan correction: the driver was changed, the notes filled in, and the reason for the correction recorded.

<Shot name="reports-audit-detail" alt="A surat jalan correction: driver from Joko Purnomo to Rahmat Hidayat, notes filled in, and the reason for the correction" />

Weights are in kg, dates as dd/mm/yyyy, and yes/no as **Yes** or **No**. A field that only holds an internal code shows its first 8 characters, such as `#0192a3b4`.

<Aside type="note" title="Nobody can change it">
The audit trail is read only. Its entries cannot be changed or deleted, not even by the Owner, and each entry carries a fingerprint of the one before it, so a quiet change would show.
</Aside>

## Period, export and print

The period is a year at most, as for the other reports. **Export xlsx** and **Export csv** include every entry matching the dates and filters, not only those loaded on screen; each change is written on its own line, such as `Driver: Joko Purnomo → Rahmat Hidayat`. **Print** <span class="pin">5</span> prints at most 5,000 entries. See [Printing and exporting reports](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/laporan/mencetak-dan-mengekspor/).
