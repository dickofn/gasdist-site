The kartu stok is the stock history of one product: every receipt, surat jalan, cancellation and adjustment, with the balance after each line. Use it to trace where a stock figure comes from. For the totals by kind, every product at once, use the [Stock summary](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/laporan/ringkasan-stok/) report: its in, out and closing figures are the kartu stok's.

<Roles can={["OWNER", "ADMIN", "GUDANG", "PENJUALAN", "VIEWER"]} />

## Opening the kartu stok

There are two ways in:

| From | How |
|---|---|
| The **Home** page | Click **Kartu stok** on the product's card. Click **Back** to return to the Home page. |
| The **Reports** menu | Type or choose the product under **Product**. Handy when you want to move from one product to another. |

### From the Home page

<Steps>

1. Click **Kartu stok** <span class="pin">3</span> on the product card.

   <Shot name="home-stock-card" alt="Product card for LPG 5,5 kg with the Kartu stok button" marks={[null, null, 3]} />

2. The product's kartu stok opens, from the 1st of this month to today. Change **From** and **To** <span class="pin">1</span> to see another period. The summary starts with the **Opening balance** <span class="pin">3</span>, and the export buttons <span class="pin">2</span> sit next to the dates.

   <Shot name="stock-card" alt="Kartu stok for LPG 3 kg from 1 to 13 October 2026 with an opening balance of 4,620 kg" />

</Steps>

### From the Reports menu

Open **Reports** in the sidebar; the **Kartu stok** tab opens first. Under **Product** <span class="pin">1</span>, type part of the product's name or code and pick it, then set **From** and **To** <span class="pin">2</span>. Inactive products stay in the list, marked **(Inactive)**. The export buttons <span class="pin">3</span> work the same as on the Home page.

When **To** is today, the **Closing balance** <span class="pin">4</span> equals the figure on the product's card on the Home page. In the picture, both are 77 kg.

<Shot name="reports-stock-card" alt="The Reports page showing the kartu stok for LPG 5,5 kg with a closing balance of 77 kg" />

## Reading the kartu stok

Above the table are four summary figures:

| Figure | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Opening balance** | Stock at the start of the period, before the first line of the table |
| **In** | Total stock in during the period |
| **Out** | Total stock out during the period |
| **Closing balance** | Stock at the end of the period. Opening balance + In − Out. |

Each row of the table is one stock movement. The **Balance** column is the stock left after that row, so you can follow the stock up and down line by line. The **By** column shows who recorded it.

| Type | Where it comes from | Effect on stock |
|---|---|---|
| **Receipt** | [Receive stock](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/stok/terima-stok/). Reference = the reference number, e.g. the DO number. | In |
| **Surat jalan** | A [posted](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/membuat/) surat jalan. Reference = the surat jalan number. | Out |
| **Return** | A [return](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/retur/) of filled tabung from a customer. Reference = the return number, e.g. `RT/2026/10/0001`. | In |
| **Cancellation** | A [cancelled](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/membatalkan/) surat jalan, or a cancelled return | In for a surat jalan, out for a return |
| **Adjustment** | A [stock opname](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/stok/stok-opname/). Its reason shows under **Note**. | In or out, following the difference |

<Aside type="note">
Rows in the kartu stok cannot be edited or deleted. Mistakes are always fixed with a new row (a cancellation or an adjustment), so the kartu stok shows a complete, honest history.
</Aside>

## Exporting the kartu stok

<Steps>

1. Set the product and the period first. The export contains exactly what is on screen.

2. Click **Export Excel** <span class="pin">2</span> for an `.xlsx` file, or **Export CSV** for a `.csv` file.

   <Shot name="stock-card" alt="The Export Excel and Export CSV buttons on the kartu stok" marks={[null, 2, null]} />

3. Your computer's save dialog opens with a file name already filled in, for example `kartu-stok-LPG-3-2026-10-01-2026-10-13.xlsx`. Choose a folder and save.

4. The message **Kartu stok saved to** appears, followed by where the file is.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Choose **Export Excel** when the file will be opened and tidied up in Excel. Choose **Export CSV** when it is going into another program.
</Aside>
