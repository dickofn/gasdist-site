Short answers to the questions office and warehouse staff ask most. Each answer links to the full guide.

<Roles can={["OWNER", "ADMIN", "GUDANG", "PENJUALAN", "VIEWER"]} />

## Can I edit a surat jalan after it is posted?

Hardly. Once posted, a surat jalan gets its number, the stock goes down and its contents are locked. This is on purpose, so the signed paper always matches the record in GasDist.

One exception: while the status is still **Posted**, the Owner or an Admin can [correct](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/mengoreksi/) its vehicle, driver and notes, with a reason. Stock does not change.

If the quantity, product or customer is wrong, the Owner or an Admin [cancels](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/membatalkan/) that surat jalan with a reason and their password. The stock comes back, and the number stays on record as cancelled. With **Cancel & recreate**, a new surat jalan form opens straight away with the same details, ready to fix.

As long as it is still a **draft**, a surat jalan can be changed or deleted freely.

## The signed sheet came back. What do I record?

Open the surat jalan and click **Mark delivered**: fill in the receiver's name, the date signed and, if you have one, a photo or scan of the sheet. The status becomes **Delivered** and is final: it can no longer be corrected or cancelled. See [Marking a surat jalan delivered](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/menandai-terkirim/).

Which sheets are still out? The **Not back yet** card on [Home](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/beranda/) gives how many; the list is under **Reports**, tab **Surat jalan not back yet**. See [Surat jalan not back yet](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/belum-kembali/).

## A customer gave back filled tabung after the surat jalan was delivered. How do I record it?

With a [return](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/retur/). A **Delivered** surat jalan cannot be cancelled, because its signed sheet is back. Open that surat jalan, click **Make a return**, enter the tabung and the reason, then **Save & number**. The stock goes up and the return gets a number of its own, such as `RT/2026/10/0002`, with a note you can print. A return is only for **full** tabung; empty tabung a pangkalan swaps go in the surat jalan's **Empties back** column.

## How many of our tabung are at the pangkalan?

Open **Reports**, the [Tabung on loan](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/laporan/tabung-dipinjam/) tab: per customer and product, the full tabung sent less the empties back, the returns and the counts.

Surat jalan from before GasDist 0.13.0 are not counted, because their empties were never recorded. After upgrading, first record each customer's opening count with **Count tabung**.

## How many tabung went to one pangkalan this month?

Open **Reports**, tab **Deliveries**, with **Group by** set to **By customer**, then type the pangkalan's name or code in **Find in the report**. There is a tabung column per product, and a **Signed back** column for the sheets that came back. Choose **By driver** or **By vehicle** to add up per driver or per truck. See [Deliveries](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/laporan/pengiriman/).

## Who changed this?

The Owner and Admin can open **Reports**, tab **Audit trail**, and filter by user, action or entity. Each entry shows who, when, and the old and new values. See [Audit trail](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/laporan/jejak-audit/).

## Are the audit trail and the stock still whole?

The Owner and Admin can open **Reports**, tab **Check data**, and click **Check now**. GasDist checks every audit entry and compares each product's stock balance with its ledger, without changing anything. If anything turns red, that screen says what to do. See [Checking the data](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/alat-data/periksa-data/).

## Can a customer list from Excel go in at once?

Yes, for the Owner or Admin: **Customers** → **Import**. Download the template, fill it in Excel, then choose the file. GasDist shows every row first and gives the reason for each refused row, for example a code already in use or a phone number typed as a number so its leading 0 is gone. See [Importing customers](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/alat-data/impor-pelanggan/).

## The accountant wants all the data. What do I give?

The Owner can make one Excel file with every table in **Settings** → **Export all data**. The file is not encrypted and holds no passwords; give it only to people entitled to it. Against damage, keep using backups. See [Exporting all data](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/alat-data/ekspor-semua-data/).

## Why does a report cover one year at most?

The stock summary, surat jalan register, deliveries and the audit trail cover at most 366 days at once, so they stay fast even with years of history. For several years, open or export the report a year at a time. The kartu stok and Surat jalan not back yet have no such limit.

## If I edit a customer or a driver, do old surat jalan change too?

No. At posting, a surat jalan keeps the customer details of that moment (code, name, address, city, phone), the plate number, the driver's name, and each product's code and name. Old surat jalan show and print as they were. Edits apply to drafts and new surat jalan only.

## I saved a surat jalan, so why has the stock not gone down?

Because what you saved is still a draft. **Save draft** does not change stock and gives no number yet. The stock goes down only after you click **Post** and confirm. See [Making a surat jalan](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/membuat/).

## Why can't I post?

There are a few possible reasons:

| What you see | Cause | Way out |
|---|---|---|
| A red line marked **Negative**, and the **Post** button does not respond | Not enough stock of that product | Lower the quantity or remove the line. If the tabung really have arrived, [record the stock receipt](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/stok/terima-stok/) first. |
| No **Post** or **New surat jalan** button at all | Your role (Warehouse or Viewer) may not make surat jalan | Ask Sales, an Admin or the Owner. |
| The message **Number ... is already used by another surat jalan** | The number format produced a number that already exists | Ask the Owner to check the [number format](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/pengaturan/format-nomor/). |

## Why is a menu or button missing on my screen?

GasDist hides the menus and buttons your role may not use. For example, Warehouse does not see **New surat jalan**, and only the Owner sees the **Settings** menu. The full list is in [User roles](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/peran/). If you really need that permission, ask an Admin or the Owner to change your role.

## Why did the screen suddenly lock?

GasDist locks the screen by itself when there has been no typing or mouse movement for a while (15 minutes by default). Type your password, then click **Unlock**. The Owner can change the time in [Settings](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/pengaturan/stok-dan-keamanan/). Anything not yet saved is not kept, so make a habit of saving the draft before you leave your desk. See [Signing in, locking and passwords](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/masuk-dan-kunci/).

## Where is my data kept?

All data is kept on the PC where GasDist is installed, not on the internet. It is encrypted, and the key is kept by that PC's operating system (on Windows: the Credential Manager of your Windows account). This means:

- Only GasDist on that PC can open the data.
- Copying the database file to another PC will not open. To move data, always use a [backup](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/backup/membuat-backup/).
- Backups go to the GasDist backup folder. Its location is shown in **Settings**, in the backup part, and the Owner can add a second folder, a USB stick for example.

## How do I install a new version? Does GasDist update itself over the internet?

No. GasDist never connects to the internet, not even to look for updates. Every new version comes with one update file (`.gasupdate`) handed over on a USB stick; the Owner installs it under **Settings › About › Update from file…**. GasDist first checks that the file genuinely comes from the GasDist publisher and is newer, and makes a backup before installing it. See [Updating GasDist](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/memperbarui/).

## A new version has a problem. Can I go back to the previous one?

Yes. On Windows, run the previous version's installer; with the AppImage, rename back the `…-previous….bak` file kept when updating. If the new version also changed how the data is stored, going back is through the backup made just before the update. See [Going back to the previous version](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/memperbarui/#going-back-to-the-previous-version).

## What if the PC breaks down or is lost?

The data can be brought back from a backup, as long as these three things were prepared beforehand:

| Preparation | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| A [backup password](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/backup/kata-sandi-backup/) is set | Without a backup password, GasDist makes no backups at all. |
| There is a backup copy outside the PC, on a USB stick for example | A backup kept only on the broken PC is lost with it. Use the second backup folder or **Save a copy to…**. See [Making and copying backups](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/backup/membuat-backup/). |
| The backup password is written down somewhere safe | GasDist cannot show it again, and a backup cannot be opened without it. |

On the new PC, install GasDist. On the first-run setup screen, do not fill in the form; click **Restore from a backup…** instead, choose the backup file and enter its backup password. See [Restoring from a backup](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/backup/memulihkan/).

## Can two PCs share the same data?

No. GasDist is built for one PC per database and does not sync data over a network. If GasDist is installed on two PCs, each has its own data, unconnected, and the two cannot be merged. To move to another PC, make a backup on the old PC and restore it on the new one.

## How do I save a surat jalan as a PDF?

<Steps>

1. Open a posted surat jalan and click **Print**. The print window opens.

2. Choose the **Paper**. Under **Sheets**, choose **All sheets** for a PDF with every labelled sheet (one PDF page per sheet), or a single sheet. Then click **Print** in that window.

3. In the system print dialog, choose a PDF printer as the destination, for example **Microsoft Print to PDF** on Windows, and save the file.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
Saving to PDF counts as one print. If you save the PDF first, the paper printed after it is marked as a copy (**SALINAN**, or **COPY** when the company language is English). See [Printing a surat jalan](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/cetak/mencetak/) and [SALINAN and DRAFT](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/cetak/salinan-dan-draf/).
</Aside>

## Why does one Print put out three sheets?

Since GasDist 0.10.0, one **Print** puts out every sheet of the surat jalan at once: by default **Lembar 1: Pelanggan** (customer), **Lembar 2: Kembali ke kantor** (back to the office) and **Lembar 3: Arsip** (archive). The three count as one print and are all originals. The Owner can change the number of sheets and their labels, or use carbon forms (one sheet), in the [print settings](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/pengaturan/cetak/). For a single print, the **Sheets** choice in the print window can also be narrowed to one sheet.

## I forgot my password. What do I do?

Ask an Admin or the Owner to reset your password. You get a temporary password that you must change when you sign in. An Owner who forgot their password can use the recovery code. See [Signing in, locking and passwords](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/masuk-dan-kunci/).
