<Roles can={["OWNER", "ADMIN", "GUDANG", "PENJUALAN", "VIEWER"]} />

## Can I edit a surat jalan after it is posted?

No. Once posted, a surat jalan gets its number, the stock goes down and its contents are locked. This is on purpose, so the signed paper always matches the record in GasDist.

If something is wrong (quantity, product or customer), the Owner or an Admin [cancels](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/membatalkan/) that surat jalan with a reason and their password. The stock comes back, and the number stays on record as cancelled. Then [make a new surat jalan](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/membuat/) with the right contents.

As long as it is still a **draft**, a surat jalan can be changed or deleted freely.

## I saved a surat jalan, so why has the stock not gone down?

Because what you saved is still a draft. **Save draft** does not change stock and gives no number yet. The stock goes down only after you click **Post** and confirm. See [Making a surat jalan](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/surat-jalan/membuat/).

## Why can't I post?

There are a few possible reasons:

| What you see | Cause | Way out |
|---|---|---|
| A red line marked **Negative**, and the **Post** button does not respond | Not enough stock of that product | Lower the quantity or remove the line. If the tabung really have arrived, [record the stock receipt](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/stok/terima-stok/) first. |
| No **Post** or **New surat jalan** button at all | Your role (Warehouse or Viewer) may not make surat jalan | Ask Sales, an Admin or the Owner. |
| The message **Number ... is already used by another surat jalan** | The number format produced a number that already exists | Ask the Owner to check the [number format](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/pengaturan/format-nomor/). |

## Why is a menu or button missing on my screen?

GasDist hides the menus and buttons your role may not use. For example, Warehouse does not see **New surat jalan**, and only the Owner sees the **Settings** menu. The full list is in [User roles](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/peran/). If you really need that permission, ask an Admin or the Owner to change your role.

## Why did the screen suddenly lock?

GasDist locks the screen by itself when there has been no typing or mouse movement for a while (15 minutes by default). Type your password, then click **Unlock**. The Owner can change the time in [Settings](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/pengaturan/stok-dan-keamanan/). Anything not yet saved is not kept, so make a habit of saving the draft before you leave your desk. See [Signing in, locking and passwords](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/masuk-dan-kunci/).

## Where is my data kept?

All data is kept on the PC where GasDist is installed, not on the internet. It is encrypted, and the key is kept by that PC's operating system (on Windows: the Credential Manager of your Windows account). This means:

- Only GasDist on that PC can open the data.
- Copying the database file to another PC will not open. To move data, always use a [backup](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/backup/membuat-backup/).
- Backups go to the GasDist backup folder. Its location is shown in **Settings**, in the backup part, and the Owner can add a second folder, a USB stick for example.

## What if the PC breaks down or is lost?

The data can be brought back from a backup, as long as these three things were prepared beforehand:

| Preparation | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| A [backup password](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/backup/kata-sandi-backup/) is set | Without a backup password, GasDist makes no backups at all. |
| There is a backup copy outside the PC, on a USB stick for example | A backup kept only on the broken PC is lost with it. Use the second backup folder or **Save a copy to…**. See [Making and copying backups](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/backup/membuat-backup/). |
| The backup password is written down somewhere safe | GasDist cannot show it again, and a backup cannot be opened without it. |

On the new PC, install GasDist. On the first-run setup screen, do not fill in the form; click **Restore from a backup…** instead, choose the backup file and enter its backup password. See [Restoring from a backup](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/backup/memulihkan/).

## Can two PCs share the same data?

No. GasDist is built for one PC per database and does not sync data over a network. If GasDist is installed on two PCs, each has its own data, unconnected, and the two cannot be merged. To move to another PC, make a backup on the old PC and restore it on the new one.

## How do I save a surat jalan as a PDF?

<Steps>

1. Open a posted surat jalan and click **Print**. The print window opens.

2. Choose the **Paper** size, then click **Print** in that window.

3. In the system print dialog, choose a PDF printer as the destination, for example **Microsoft Print to PDF** on Windows, and save the file.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
Saving to PDF counts as one print. If you save the PDF first, the paper printed after it is marked as a copy (**SALINAN**, or **COPY** when the company language is English). See [Printing a surat jalan](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/cetak/mencetak/) and [SALINAN and DRAFT](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/cetak/salinan-dan-draf/).
</Aside>

## I forgot my password. What do I do?

Ask an Admin or the Owner to reset your password. You get a temporary password that you must change when you sign in. An Owner who forgot their password can use the recovery code. See [Signing in, locking and passwords](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/masuk-dan-kunci/).
