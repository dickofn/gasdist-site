The company details print at the top of every surat jalan, so make sure they are correct and complete. All of this is set on the **Settings** page, which appears in the sidebar for the Owner only.

<Roles can={["OWNER"]} />

## Changing the company details

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar. The **Company** tab opens first.

   <Shot name="settings-company" alt="The Settings page on the Company tab: name, address, phone, NPWP and logo" marks={[1, 2, 3]} />

2. Change the **Address**, **Phone** or logo as needed. **Company name** <span class="pin">1</span> and **NPWP** follow the license and are locked (see below); until a license is added, they can be changed here too.

3. Click **Save** in the bar that appears at the bottom of the screen. The message **Settings saved.** appears.

</Steps>

The bar shows up as soon as you change something and reads **You have unsaved changes.** **Cancel** puts every field back to what is saved.

<Shot name="settings-save-bar" alt="The bar at the bottom of Settings: You have unsaved changes, with the Cancel and Save buttons" marks={false} />

<Aside type="note">
One **Save** button covers every tab: company details, the [number format and printing](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/pengaturan/format-nomor/), [negative stock, the automatic lock and language](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/pengaturan/stok-dan-keamanan/), and the backup folder. Moving from one tab to another does not throw changes away. If **Save** is refused because a field is wrong, GasDist opens the tab that holds the mistake. If you leave the page before clicking **Save**, your changes are not kept.
</Aside>

### The company name and NPWP from the license

While a license is installed, **Company name** <span class="pin">1</span> and **NPWP** <span class="pin">2</span> come from the license file and cannot be changed. Under both it says **From the license. To change it, contact your GasDist seller.** <span class="pin">3</span>

<Shot name="settings-company-locked" alt="The Company section: Company name and NPWP locked with the note From the license" />

If the company name or NPWP changes, for example with a new deed, contact your GasDist seller for a replacement license file. See [License and trial](/gasdist-site/en/panduan/mulai/lisensi/#the-company-name-and-npwp-are-locked).

### Where these details show

| Field | Shows on |
|---|---|
| **Company name** | The top of the surat jalan, the top-left corner of the GasDist window, and the restore window for backups |
| **Address** | The top of the surat jalan, under the company name |
| **Phone** and **NPWP** | The top of the surat jalan, on one line under the address. Empty ones are not printed |

## Adding a logo

<Steps>

1. Click **Choose logo** <span class="pin">2</span> on the **Company** tab.

2. A file picker opens that shows only PNG and JPEG files. Pick the logo file and confirm.

3. The logo shows above the buttons and is used straight away. For the logo you do not need to click **Save**.

</Steps>

The logo must be a **PNG or JPEG image of at most 512 KB**. Other files are refused with a message saying the logo must be a PNG or JPEG image. The logo prints to the left of the company name at the top of the surat jalan.

To change the logo, click **Choose logo** again and pick a new file. To drop it, click **Remove logo**. Without a logo, the section says **No logo yet. The logo prints at the top of the surat jalan.**

<Aside type="tip">
A logo on a white or transparent background looks neatest on paper. If your logo file is over 512 KB, make it smaller first in an image editor.
</Aside>
